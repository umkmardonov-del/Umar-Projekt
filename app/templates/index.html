System Configurator

How it works

Select department, team size and your preferred setup. The tool calculates a practical workstation recommendation including hardware, operating system and software package.

It helps you compare scenarios quickly (for example Desktop vs. Laptop or Office vs. Developer Tools) and gives you a transparent result basis for planning decisions.

Use presets for a quick start, then fine-tune values to match your real team situation before running the calculation.

Account Security

Risk Level: Low

How it works

This score uses Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability (CIA). Each selection contributes to the total risk score and updates the risk level immediately.

Low values mean lower protection needs, while high values indicate stronger security controls should be planned for the account or system.
